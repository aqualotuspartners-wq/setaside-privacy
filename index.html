Privacy policy for Setaside

1099 Tax Calculator: Setaside (“Setaside”, “the app”). Effective November 24, 2026. This policy describes the Android app published by Aqua Lotus Partners.

What stays on your phone

The amounts you enter stay on the device. That includes 1099 income, business expenses, mileage, quarterly payments, filing status, state, an optional effective state rate, optional W-2 wages, federal withholding, other income, and prior-year tax figures you type for the safe-harbor note.

Those entries are stored in a local database on the phone. Setaside has no account, no sign-in, and no server run by Aqua Lotus Partners. The app does not connect to a bank, does not file a tax return, and does not move money. Your financial entries are not sold, and they are not sent to Aqua Lotus Partners.

Android backup and device transfer of this app’s data are turned off. If you use Export CSV, the files go only where you send them with the share sheet. Uninstalling the app deletes the entries stored by the app on that device.

Advertising and consent

Setaside shows ads through Google AdMob so the app can be free. Before an ad is requested, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent where Google requires it. A banner may appear on the home screen and on lists. A full-screen ad may appear at most once in a session, only after you save an entry, and not when you first open the app. Ads are not placed on the screens where you type amounts.

If ads are allowed, Google can receive the Advertising ID, IP address, and device information it uses to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. That processing is governed by Google’s policies, not by an account with Aqua Lotus Partners. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings, and you can limit ad personalization in Google’s ads settings. If you do not consent, the calculator still works; ads are not loaded.

The app declares the Advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) for this purpose. It also uses internet access and network-state access for ads, the consent message, and for links you choose to open (the privacy policy and the IRS or SSA sources).

Reminders

If you turn on quarterly reminders, the app schedules local notifications about seven days and one day before each estimated-tax due date. Notification permission is requested only when you turn that on. Reminders are timed on the device. They are not messages from the IRS, and the dates and amounts are not uploaded to us. After a reboot, the app can reschedule those reminders.

Children

Setaside is not directed to children under 13, and it is a tool for adults who have self-employment income. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
